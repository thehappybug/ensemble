# Ideal customer profile

We target developer-tool companies building coding-agent products. Prefer:

- A public API or developer SDK, documented with a primary-source URL.
- Roughly 20–500 employees and Series A–C funding. Treat unverified headcount or
  funding as unknown, and distinguish estimates from reported facts.
- Evidence of SDK, API, integration, developer-experience, or platform investment
  during the past six months.
- An identifiable product manager or engineering leader with public evidence of
  responsibility for the relevant platform or developer experience.

Score each company from 0 to 100: API/SDK need (35), recent investment signals
(30), company stage and size (20), and reachable relevant buyer (15). Include the
component scores, uncertainty, sources, and a concrete reason to contact them.
Missing data must lower confidence rather than become an invented fact.

For the live cascade, add the following criterion to this file: "Must have a
public changelog." The fit and ranking stages must reuse existing research;
research about people and product direction should remain fresh.
